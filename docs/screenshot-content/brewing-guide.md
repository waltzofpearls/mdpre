# Brewing Guide

How to get consistent results from every method.

## Grind Size

Grind size is the single biggest lever on extraction. Start here before adjusting anything else.

| Method | Grind | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Espresso | Fine | Adjust in small steps, taste after each |
| Pour Over | Medium | Even particle size matters more than fineness |
| French Press | Coarse | Too fine and the plunger will stall |
| Cold Brew | Extra coarse | Long steep, low agitation |

## Water Temperature

Aim for 92 to 96 degrees Celsius for hot methods. Off boil is close enough for most beans.

Darker roasts tolerate the lower end of the range. Lighter roasts need the heat to extract fully.

## Ratio

The ratio is expressed as coffee to water by weight.

```sh
brewlab ratio --method pour-over
# 1:15
```

## Troubleshooting

If the cup tastes sour, the extraction was too short. Grind finer or extend the brew.
If it tastes bitter, the extraction ran long. Grind coarser or pull it sooner.
