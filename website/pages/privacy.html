Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 August 2026

The short version

Markdown Preview does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Your documents never leave your Mac.

Your documents

Files you open are read from your Mac and rendered on your Mac. They are never uploaded anywhere. The app has no server, and there is no service behind it to send anything to.

Folder access

When the app needs to read a folder, macOS asks you to confirm it. Your choice is stored locally on your Mac so you are not asked again for that folder. This record never leaves the device, and you can clear it by removing the app.

Text to speech

Speech is produced by the voices built into macOS, on your device. The text of your document is passed to the system speech engine and is not sent to Markdown Preview or to any third party.

Document statistics

Word, character, and token counts are calculated on your Mac using a tokenizer bundled inside the app. The estimated cost figures are arithmetic against published prices. No network request is made, and no third party AI service is contacted.

Software updates

The direct download version (.dmg) checks for updates, which means a periodic request to GitHub for an update feed. GitHub receives the same information any website receives from a visit, such as your IP address. No identifier is attached and nothing about your documents is included. The Mac App Store version has no update checker at all, since updates come from the App Store.

The App Store

If you install from the Mac App Store, Apple provides me with aggregate statistics such as download and update counts, and with crash reports if you have chosen to share diagnostics with developers. This information comes from Apple rather than from the app, contains no document content, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy rather than this one.

This website

This site is static, serves no cookies, and embeds nothing from third parties. The hosting provider keeps standard server logs.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, or delete personal data are difficult to exercise here because there is none to act on. Nothing is collected, so there is no account to close, no record to request, and no data to export or erase.

Children

Markdown Preview is a document viewer for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
